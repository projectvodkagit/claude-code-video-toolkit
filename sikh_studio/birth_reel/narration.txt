In fourteen sixty-nine, in Rai Bhoi di Talwandi, Punjab, a son was born to Tripta and Mehta Kalu. Kalu worked as a village accountant. The child was named Nanak. Later Sikh biographical accounts describe a midwife named Daulatan at the birth. They also describe an astrologer named Hardial visiting the family afterward. These details come from the Janamsakhi tradition. They are not verified by contemporary eyewitness records. Talwandi is now called Nankana Sahib, in present-day Pakistan. The child became known to history as Guru Nanak, the first Sikh Guru.
