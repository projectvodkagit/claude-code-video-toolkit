What made Guru Nanak Dev Ji different from the beginning? History and tradition offer different kinds of answers. He was born in 1469, in Talwandi, to Mata Tripta and Mehta Kalu. A later Janamsakhi account describes an unusual sound at his birth, but this is tradition, not a provable event. Another traditional story tells of a sacred-thread ceremony in his childhood. Rather than accepting the symbol without question, Nanak asked what it truly meant. The teaching associated with this story focuses on compassion, contentment and truth. What stands out is not a miracle we can verify. It is a message: character matters more than appearance.
